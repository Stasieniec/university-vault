# MNLP L06 Contextual Embeddings: numbers taken off the flashcards (2026-10-08)

Raw material for the exam cheat sheet. Values as stated in the lecture note.

- BERT-base: 110M parameters, 12 layers, hidden 768, 12 heads. BERT-large: 340M, 24 layers, 1024, 16 heads.
- BERT pre-training: batch 256 x 512 tokens; BookCorpus 800M + English Wikipedia 2.5B tokens.
- BERT fine-tuning: batch 16 or 32, 2 to 4 epochs.
- mBERT about 178M parameters (BERT-base 110M); vocabulary 120k (BERT 30k).
- XLM-R: CC-100, 2.5TB, 100 languages; vocabulary 250k.
- Sampling with alpha = 0.7, English 1000 vs Swahili 10: p_sw = 0.038 (0.0099 at alpha = 1); 100:1 becomes about 25:1.
- Whole-word masking, BERT-Large uncased: SQuAD F1 91.0 to 92.8; MultiNLI 86.05 to 87.07.
- Pires et al. NER: EN to NL 77.36 vs in-language NL 89.86. Cross-script POS: HI to UR 85.9, UR to HI 91.1, EN to BG 87.1, EN to JA 49.4.
- Pires et al. word order (POS): SVO to SVO 81.55 vs SVO to SOV 66.52.
- Pires et al. translation retrieval: peaks 71-76% in layers 6-8 (EN-DE about 76 at layer 8).
- Wu & Dredze MLDoc zero-shot average: mBERT 74.5, Artetxe & Schwenk 74.9; in-language 91.2.
- Wu & Dredze XNLI average: mBERT zero-shot 66.3, translate-train 71.6, XLM (MLM) 71.5, XLM (MLM+TLM) 75.1.
- Wu & Dredze NER zero-shot average: mBERT 74.03 vs Xie et al. 67.13.
- Wu & Dredze POS: mBERT zero-shot 84.3 vs Kim et al. with 320 sentences 89.9.
- Wu & Dredze best freezing level: layer 0/3 NER, 3 POS, 6 MLDoc and XNLI. Freezing up to layer 9: NER 67.3 vs 74.3.
- Wu & Dredze overlap correlation R (type level): NER 0.99, MLDoc 0.8, POS 0.58, parsing 0.5, XNLI -0.036.
- K et al. fake English, no subword overlap: costs 0.5 to 1.4 XNLI (en-es 1.4, en-hi 0.5, en-ru 0.7).
- K et al. full word permutation: XNLI drops 8.4 (es), 16.5 (hi), 12.1 (ru).
- K et al. depth: in-language minus Russian gap 21.6 at 1 layer, 11.3 at 24 layers.
- K et al. mixed-language XNLI 45.7 to 61.1 vs monolingual 59.6 to 79.3.
- Rajaee & Monz XNLI: mBERT within 65.7, across 54.5; XLM-R within 74.2, across 64.8.
- Rajaee & Monz XSQuAD F1: mBERT within 64.4, across 44.2; XLM-R within 72.0, across 36.8.
- SNLI: full premise-hypothesis word overlap means entailment 94.7% of the time.
