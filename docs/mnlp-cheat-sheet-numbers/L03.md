# MNLP L03 Morphology and Word Formation: numbers taken off the flashcards (2026-10-08)

Raw material for the exam cheat sheet. Values as stated in the lecture note.

- Word embedding dimension: e.g. 512, against a vocabulary of hundreds of thousands.
- CBOW context: n = m, n in {2, 5, 10}.
- Realistic vocabulary sizes: English about 200K words, Russian about 1M (about five to one).
- Typical short-list sizes: 10K, 50K, 100K, sometimes 200K.
- Segmentations of an n-character string: 2^(n-1); 524,288 for 20 characters.
- Forward maximum matching: O(n·L) dictionary lookups.
- Viterbi: O(n|T|^2), 16n for BMES; brute force |T|^n (4^n).
- Parameters of a k-th order HMM grow as |T|^(k+1).
- Forms per lemma: English noun 2; x6 cases = 12; x6 possessive persons = 72.
